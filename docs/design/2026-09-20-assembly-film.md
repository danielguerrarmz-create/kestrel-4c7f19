# Assembly film direction

The current sequence is an interactive model study, not the finished film. A convincing film should be rendered from the actual engine geometry so timber and connections remain consistent from frame to frame.

## Proposed 12-second shot

- 0-2 seconds: quiet warm mineral ground plane, support positions visible, fixed three-quarter camera.
- 2-7 seconds: identified timber groups ease into their final positions. Stagger arrivals; show connections seating rather than dissolving entire rows into existence.
- 7-9 seconds: completed structure holds; a restrained camera move reveals depth and joint relationships.
- 9-12 seconds: planting appears around the supports, with believable scale and variation. Hold the finished composition before replay.

## Production

Export member solids and connectors from Bower Engine with member IDs intact. Assemble in Blender, bevel timber edges, align grain with each member, use restrained galvanized steel and broad soft daylight with contact shadows. Replace schematic leaves with modeled planting. Avoid floating pieces, intersecting members, oversaturated timber and arbitrary camera orbits. Review assembly grouping with engineering before describing it as a construction method.

Approve one rendered still before rendering the film. Then review a low-resolution motion preview. Deliver a 24 fps desktop film and a separately framed mobile version, MP4/WebM plus poster. Keep the structure large and legible at 390 px width. The existing scrubber can later drive video currentTime; pause offscreen and retain a static poster for reduced motion.
