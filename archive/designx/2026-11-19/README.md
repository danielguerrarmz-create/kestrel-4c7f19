# Archived DesignX editions

Archived on 19 November 2026. The active presentations are /designx and /designx/v10/. Retail remains active at /cre-retail.

Editions 1 to 6, v7 and v8 have their entry documents and edition-specific PDF downloads here. Shared scripts, drawings, fonts and media stay under public/designx because the active presentations import them. This archive is outside the published public directory.

To restore an edition, move its files back to the matching public/designx/<edition>/ paths and restore its aliases in src/documentRoutes.ts and vercel.json. Original file contents are retained.
