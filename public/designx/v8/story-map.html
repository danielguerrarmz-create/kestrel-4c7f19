One continuous story about bringing a living place into being.

We encounter a Bower and understand why someone would want it. Looking more closely reveals the relationships that give it its character. Designing those relationships raises decisions that computation helps us explore. Those decisions produce particular parts and manufacturing requirements. Bringing the place into someone’s garden requires a credible commission and delivery process. Its continuing life requires care, and that experience informs the next Bower.

V7 provides the visual system and source scenes. V8 gives Time, Light and Space separate autoplaying drawings, adds a duplicate Time camera comparison, follows a consequential design change through the scene export, explains the separate making/growth/budget engine and preserves Daniel’s factory drawings. The original learning flowchart remains on slide 17; a separate question-led comparison follows on slide 18, with a calmer hierarchy and progressively revealed detail. The twenty-two-slide sequence brings prospective customers in after manufacturing, followed by the international customer-discovery finding and the commission offer. Ground and care remain separate drawing commissions. Pre-rendered scenarios, live SVG process drawings and authored explanations have distinct capabilities and evidence labels.
